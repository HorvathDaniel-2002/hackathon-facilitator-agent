# Use the materials - no installation needed

These files let you plan and run the workshop manually. **You do not need the
agent, GitHub Copilot, Node.js or an Azure account.** Use an application that can
open Word/PowerPoint files, or read the PDFs in your browser.

## Start in five minutes

1. Download the Word playbook, or its PDF for reading/printing.
2. Save a **working copy in your approved team location**. Replace the bracketed
   prompts; duplicate the use-case, test and handoff pages for each team.
3. Open the PowerPoint to lead the conversation. Open **Notes** or **Presenter
   View** for activity prompts and source references. Adapt it to your audience.
4. Ask the sponsor, business owner and IT/data contacts to resolve missing
   decisions. Record a real owner, date and evidence instead of assuming approval.

For manual use, the files are the tool. The optional Copilot agent is only another
way of helping with the same planning process.

## What's inside the playbook?

- Pages 1-3: getting started, charter and one-use-case worksheet.
- Pages 4-5: eight qualification conditions and an optional, evidence-based manual rubric.
- Pages 6-7: all 13 start checks with evidence-record templates.
- Pages 8-10: sample two-/three-day event rhythm, blockers, decisions and six test categories.
- Pages 11-12: exit packages, four closure checks, cleanup and value measurement.
- Pages 13-14: fictional example, optional prompt, glossary and public Microsoft references.

## Use safely

All examples are fictional. A description, score or demo is not permission,
production certification or measured business value. Schedules, weights and checks
are project-authored defaults, not official Microsoft corporate requirements.
Follow your organization's current rules and confirm the actual approver.

Public templates do not make completed workbooks public: **do not commit or upload
customer details, internal documents, approval evidence, raw prompt logs or completed
plans to this repository or its issues.** These files contain no macros or app
connections, but your own edits still need appropriate classification and sharing.
